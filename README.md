# OmniStock

A mobile inventory and billing management app built with React Native,Expo and SQLite.

## About

OmniStock helps small business owners manage products, inventory, stock levels, and customer billing from a single mobile application.

The app was designed for local stores such as:

* Kirana(grocery) Stores
* Saree(A indian cloth) Shops
* Clothing Stores
* General Retail Businesses
* Small Buisnnes owners
* Your ai ecommerce platform

Users can add products, generate bills, track inventory, monitor low-stock items, and view billing history and manage ur buissnees overall.

## Features

### Store Setup

* First-time onboarding flow
* Store name 
* Business template selection(tbh it doesnt matter)

### Inventory Management

* Add new products
* Edit products
* Delete products
* Product categories
* Search products
* Category filtering
* Low stock threshold tracking

### Billing System

* Create bills/invoices
* Add products to cart
* Quantity controls
* GST calculation
* Multiple payment methods

  * Cash
  * Card
  * UPI
* Customer details

  * Name
  * Phone Number
  * Address
* Automatic stock reduction after sale

### Billing History

* View all invoices
* Customer information
* Invoice details
* Purchased items
* Payment method
* Invoice totals

### Dashboard

* Total Products
* Inventory Value
* Total Bills
* Low Stock Alerts
* Quick Actions
